About VoltPro

Powering Kenya's electrical and security needs

We are a licensed and insured electrical contractor providing comprehensive power and security solutions across Nairobi and beyond.

VoltPro Electrodata Solutions has been at the forefront of electrical and security installations, serving residential, commercial, and industrial clients with excellence and reliability.

Our team of certified technicians brings decades of combined experience in electrical wiring, solar installations, access control systems, CCTV security, and emergency power solutions. We pride ourselves on delivering quality workmanship, adhering to safety standards, and providing exceptional customer service.

From site surveys to system design, installation to maintenance, we handle every aspect of your electrical and security needs with precision and professionalism. Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted partner for businesses and homeowners throughout Kenya.

How we work

Four stages, same order every time

  1. 01

    Site survey

    We walk the site, check the existing supply and loads, and confirm what's actually feasible before we quote.

  2. 02

    Design & quote

    A written scope and single-line diagram where relevant, priced against materials and labour — no verbal estimates.

  3. 03

    Install & test

    Installation to code, with load and continuity testing before anything is energised for use.

  4. 04

    Handover & support

    Documentation, a walkthrough of your system, and a direct line for servicing or emergency call-out.
