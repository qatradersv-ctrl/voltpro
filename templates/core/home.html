How we work

Four stages, same order every time

  1. 01

    Site survey

    We walk the site, check the existing supply and loads, and confirm what's actually feasible before we quote.

  2. 02

    Design & quote

    A written scope and single-line diagram where relevant, priced against materials and labour — no verbal estimates.

  3. 03

    Install & test

    Installation to code, with load and continuity testing before anything is energised for use.

  4. 04

    Handover & support

    Documentation, a walkthrough of your system, and a direct line for servicing or emergency call-out.
