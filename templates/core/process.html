Stage 01

Site survey & assessment

We walk the site, check the existing supply and loads, and confirm what is actually feasible before we quote. Our technicians assess your current infrastructure, identify potential challenges, and determine the optimal solution for your specific needs.

This is also where a bad idea costs nothing: if the panel cannot take the load, or the roof aspect will not support the array you had in mind, you hear it here rather than in an invoice.

  • You getA measured note of existing supply, board and loads.
  • We checkEarthing, protection devices, cable routes and access.
  • What changesThe scope is fixed before anything is ordered.

Stage 02

Design & quote

A written scope and single-line diagram where relevant, priced against materials and labour — no verbal estimates. We provide detailed documentation outlining exactly what will be done, the materials to be used, and the total cost.

You can share it with a consultant, a landlord or a finance approver without needing us in the room.

  • Line by lineMaterials, units and labour shown separately.
  • DrawingsSingle-line diagram for panels and changeovers.
  • ValidityAn issued quote with a stated expiry, not an open figure.

Stage 03

Install & test

Installation to code, with load and continuity testing before anything is energised for use. Our certified technicians follow strict safety protocols and industry standards, and every installation undergoes rigorous testing to ensure proper operation, safety compliance and optimal performance.

Sites are left tidy and labelled. If isolation is needed while we work, you are told in advance.

  • Own crewNo unvetted sub-contractors behind the panel.
  • TestingLoad and continuity checks before energising.
  • LabellingCircuits and boards identified as built.

Stage 04

Handover & support

Documentation, a walkthrough of your system, and a direct line for servicing or emergency call-out. We hand over manuals, warranties and maintenance guidance, walk you through operation, and stay reachable for the life of the installation.

Preventive servicing can be scheduled against the same record, so the next visit does not start from zero.

  • WalkthroughWhere it isolates, what to watch, what not to touch.
  • RecordsManuals, test results and maintenance guidance.
  • On callServicing and 24/7 emergency response.
