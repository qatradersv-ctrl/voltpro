Our Process

A clear path from concept to completion

We believe in transparency and structure. Every project follows these four essential stages to ensure nothing is overlooked and expectations are met.

  1. 01

    Site survey

    We walk the site, check the existing supply and loads, and confirm what's actually feasible before we quote. Our technicians assess your current infrastructure, identify potential challenges, and determine the optimal solution for your specific needs. This thorough assessment ensures accurate pricing and realistic timelines.

  2. 02

    Design & quote

    A written scope and single-line diagram where relevant, priced against materials and labour — no verbal estimates. We provide detailed documentation outlining exactly what will be done, the materials to be used, and the total cost. This transparency eliminates surprises and ensures you know exactly what you're getting.

  3. 03

    Install & test

    Installation to code, with load and continuity testing before anything is energised for use. Our certified technicians follow strict safety protocols and industry standards. Every installation undergoes rigorous testing to ensure proper operation, safety compliance, and optimal performance before handover.

  4. 04

    Handover & support

    Documentation, a walkthrough of your system, and a direct line for servicing or emergency call-out. We provide comprehensive documentation including manuals, warranties, and maintenance guides. Our team walks you through your new system, explaining operation and maintenance. Plus, you get direct access to our support team for ongoing assistance.
